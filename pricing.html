Policies & reminders

Good to know before you come

Booking & cancellation

  • Book your class through WhatsApp, Instagram or a phone call. All classes are paid in advance.
  • For your first pilates class, arrive 10 minutes early so your instructor can explain the machine and equipment.
  • If you're new to pilates, you can only book beginner classes. After an assessment, your instructor can advise you to change level.
  • Cancel or rebook with at least 12 hours' notice and no fee applies. Classes cancelled within 6 hours of the start are not refunded or transferred.
  • Packages are non-refundable and non-transferable, and must be used within a month. For travel or a medical issue, you can freeze your package for up to two weeks.

Studio etiquette

  • Grip socks are mandatory in all pilates classes. Bring your own or buy them at the studio.
  • Outdoor shoes aren't allowed in the pilates rooms. Shoe lockers are at the entrance of every room.
  • Please put your phone on silent and away during class. If you'd like to record, discuss it with the rest of the class first.
  • Be at the studio 5 minutes before class starts. If you're more than 10 minutes late, you won't be able to join.

Health & safety

  • If you're feeling unwell or have cold or flu symptoms, please stay home to protect the rest of the community.
  • Tell your instructor about any pre-existing injuries, medical conditions or pregnancy before taking part.
  • Wear suitable fitness clothing, and avoid items with sharp edges, zips or clasps that can damage equipment.
  • If you're injured during a session, tell your instructor straight away and follow their advice to modify or stop.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

I've never done pilates. Where do I start?

Book a beginner class. It's the only level open to newcomers, and your instructor will tell you when you're ready to move up. Arrive 10 minutes early for your first class so they can show you the machine.

What should I wear and bring?

Fitted workout clothes without zips, clasps or sharp edges, which can damage the equipment. Grip socks are required in every class. Bring your own or buy a pair at the studio.

How long do packages last?

Packages must be used within a month. If you're travelling or have a medical issue, you can freeze your package for up to two weeks. Packages can't be refunded or transferred.

What if I'm running late?

Please be there 5 minutes before class starts. If you're more than 10 minutes late, you won't be able to join the class.

I have an injury or I'm pregnant. Can I join?

Tell your instructor before class about any pre-existing injury, medical condition or pregnancy, so they can adapt the session or advise you. If you feel unwell or have cold or flu symptoms, please stay home.

Can I book through The Well-Being Collective app?

Yes. Studio Plume is a partner studio on TWC, so members can book with their credits in the app. Everyone else can book on WhatsApp, Instagram or by phone.
