Five ways to train at Plume

Every class is an hour, taught in small groups at beginner, intermediate or advanced level. If you've never done pilates, book a beginner class and your instructor will guide you from there.

Reformer

BeginnerIntermediateAdvanced60 minutesSmall group

The reformer is a sliding carriage held by springs. Pushing and pulling against that resistance builds strength while lengthening the body, and the springs can be set lighter or heavier for any level.

  • Releases tension: eases tight muscles and stress, so you leave refreshed rather than worn out
  • Clears your head: focused breathing resets your nervous system
  • Beginner friendly: even if you've never worked out before, it starts at your level
  • Can be adapted when you're returning from injury. Tell your instructor before class.

Mat Pilates & Mobility Flow

BeginnerIntermediate60 minutes

Classic pilates on the mat, using your own body weight with small props, plus mobility flow sessions that work on how freely you move.

  • Full-body benefits: strength, flexibility, balance and mobility trained at once
  • Gentle on joints: low impact and adaptable for every level
  • Stress relief that's also fitness: mindful breathing and focused movement
  • More energy for your day: you leave refreshed, not drained

Wunda Chair

BeginnerIntermediate60 minutes

A compact box with a sprung pedal. Small to look at, but it asks a lot of your core and balance, and it's a favourite cross-training tool for runners and dancers.

  • Posture reset for modern life: counters hours of sitting at a desk or on a phone
  • Deep core activation: abs, lower back and pelvic floor in every movement
  • Strength for daily movement: walking, standing, lifting and climbing stairs
  • Athletic conditioning: a strong cross-training tool for runners, dancers and athletes

Cadillac

Beginner60 minutesSmallest groups

Also called the trapeze table: a padded table inside a frame of bars, springs and straps. It supports you through movements you couldn't do on the floor, which makes it good both for precise first sessions and for deeper stretching.

  • Supported, precise work with springs from above and below
  • Hanging and stretching work you can't do on other equipment
  • Our Cadillac classes are kept at beginner level
