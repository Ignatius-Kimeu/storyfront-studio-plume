Your first class, step by step

Request a beginner class

Message us on WhatsApp, Instagram or by phone, or use the form. Classes are paid in advance. If pilates is new to you, start with a beginner class.

Arrive 10 minutes early

Your instructor walks you through the machine before class. Bring grip socks or buy a pair at the studio. Outdoor shoes stay in the lockers.

Move up when you're ready

After assessing you, your instructor tells you when to try intermediate or advanced classes. Packs of 4, 8 or 12 classes bring the price down.
