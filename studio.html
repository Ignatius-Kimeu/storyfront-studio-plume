Our story

Pilates for the Nyari, Muthaiga, Gigiri and Runda side of town

Studio Plume is a women-owned pilates studio in Annex 2 of Rosslyn Square Luxury Mall. It's built around three rooms: one for reformers under a glowing LED ceiling, one for Cadillac trapeze tables, and a row of Wunda chairs facing tall arched mirrors.

Classes stay small, every level is covered from first-timer to advanced, and members say the same thing in their reviews: friendly, knowledgeable instructors and a warm, welcoming room.
